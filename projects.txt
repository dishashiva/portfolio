4. AgriConnect: A google solutions challenge project for connecting farmers and market. both can view live trends of vegetables and past history via trend chart. farmers can list their vegetables for selling, markets can respond by creating an offer. farmers have an inbuilt ai powered crop health detector that takes more info from metadata such as irrigation cycle, soil type, harvest information, fertilizers, etc. just from a picture it detects the diseases and suggests fixes. 
- GitHub: GitHub.com/Dqrshan/agri-connect
- Demo: agricon.netlify.app
