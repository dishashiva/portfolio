# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and fellow developers evaluating a student developer's capabilities, portfolio, and professional trajectory. They are typically scanning for technical depth, design sensibility, and communication clarity.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio website that acts as a living résumé, showcasing projects, skills, and experience. The goal is to create a memorable, confident first impression that demonstrates both design taste and engineering capability.

## Brand Personality

- **Curious & Capable**: Disha is early in her career but shows growth, ambition, and discipline.
- **Aspirational & Organized**: The site is meant to feel like a cool startup product, not a student project.
- **Confident Coding with Style**: A high-energy "developer who gets it" vibe, not stiff or corporate. The tone is approachable and impressive.

## Anti-references

- Corporate / overly serious / no "B.E student", education-first, muted-boring developer portfolio
- Dark mode heavy SaaS template with purple/blue generic colors
- Wall of text with bad hierarchy

## Design Principles

- **Wow with Visuals**: The design is the portfolio's personality. Use energetic colors, smooth animations, and playful interactions to show frontend skill without needing words.
- **Mobile-First Impact**: Recruiters often browse on mobile first. Every viewport size needs a clean, powerful layout.
- **Personality Over Formality**: As a student, the site shouldn't feel like a Fortune 500 brand. It should feel like Disha's best self.
- **Clear Hierarchy**: Make the most important facts (who you are, what you build) instantly scannable.
- **Accessible but Bold**: Don't sacrifice personality for accessibility, but ensure all content is readable and navigable for everyone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Curated accessible standards: Ensure smooth tab navigation, ARIA labels for interactive icons, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support for the animated elements. Aim for dynamic contrast without sacrificing the vibrant palette.
